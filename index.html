The Zero-Contact Trading Protocol

We act as the anonymous, armored buffer between buyer and seller. No dangerous face-to-face meetings, no identity leaks.

1

Secure Deposit

The buyer deposits funds into the Armordillo Hub escrow system via secure EFT or card. Funds are locked safely.

2

Proxy Shipping

The seller ships the electronic item to an Armordillo verification node using our integrated locker-to-locker/courier system.

3

Hardware Audit

Our experts verify that the device matches the condition, specifications, and hardware diagnostics promised by the seller.

4

Payout & Forward

Once verified, funds are instantly cleared to the seller, and the device is forwarded safely to the buyer's destination.
