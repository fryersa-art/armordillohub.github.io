Welcome to Armordillo Hub SA – The Armoured Marketplace for Secure Tech! 🛡️⚡

Tired of fake electronic scams, fraudulent electronic fund transfers (EFTs), and dangerous meetups? You are in the right place. Armordillo Hub is South Africa's zero-scam zone for buying and selling electronics.

We act as your platform's protective shell. To guarantee 100% safety, we run a strict Admin Escrow System for all transactions in this group.

How it works:

  1. The Deal: Buyer and seller agree on a price for an item.
  2. The Shell (Secure Hold): The buyer pays the funds directly to the verified Armordillo Hub Admin account. We securely hold the money.
  3. The Handover: The seller ships the item via an approved courier (e.g., Pudo) to an anonymous locker or address.
  4. The Release: Once the buyer inspects the device and confirms it is exactly as advertised, we release the payout to the seller instantly.

No fake proof of payment scams. No ghosting. No risk.
